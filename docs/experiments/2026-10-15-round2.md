- **The hunger fixes make no measurable difference** to survival or to the share of
  deaths from starvation, even though hungry people standing on food now eat it
  53-80% of the time (was 14-30%). Eating decisions weren't the binding limit.
- **What starvation really tracks is winter.** Across the `v2` worlds, food on the
  map falls from about 700 items to 229 in winter and deaths per day double (0.10 to
  0.20). Fat holds about 8 days of energy; winter lasts 91.
- **Calls:** in all 16 worlds the sound a person makes carries slightly more
  information about their situation than chance (mutual information above the
  shuffled 95th percentile), but only about 0.008 bits of a possible 1.58, and
  listeners don't move toward calls (47%, near chance). No real signalling yet, with
  or without fear in the tone.

## The big finding: the population isn't viable

In the 10-year runs the population falls from about 31 in year 1 to about 5 by year
5, and every world spends time at the floor of 2 people that automatic top-ups
guarantee: those top-ups hide near-extinctions. Births fall below deaths from the
first year (seed 0: 23 births and 53 deaths in year 1; 1 to 8 births a year from
year 4). Two-year batches looked healthy only because founders were still alive.

Likely causes, to be tested next: a two-parent requirement with partners within 8
tiles (sparse people rarely meet, so births collapse as numbers fall: an Allee
effect), the energy needed to have a child (70%, rarely reached in winter), and few
children per lifetime relative to a 9-month life expectancy.
